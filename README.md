# CATHeaPS
**C**entralisation **A**nalysis **T**ool for **Hea**t **P**ump **S**ystems.

CATHeaPS is an open-access modeling tool designed to assess the techno-environmental-economic impacts of decarbonising thermal systems. Developed through a collaboration between the University of Glasgow and the University of Edinburgh, the tool empowers local councils, community energy groups, and stakeholders to evaluate heat network feasibility and retrofit strategies using publicly available data.

## Model overview

The transition to net-zero heating requires a clear understanding of how different technologies perform in specific urban contexts. CATHeaPS 3.0 bridges the gap between high-level policy and implementation by utilising open access Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) data to simulate various thermal system scenarios over a 40 year period. CATHeaPS 3.0 performs a complete network routing exercise based on the linear heat density of a project area, providing preliminary insights into the costs and technical requirements of shifting away from fossil fuels.

## Evaluated Thermal Systems

CATHeaPS 3.0 compares four primary configurations to provide a comprehensive view of decarbonisation options: 
- 4th Generation District Heating: Low-temperature centralised heat distribution.
- Ambient Networks: Decentralised booster heat pumps integrated into a shared ambient temperature network with a centralised balancing unit.
- Individual Air Source Heat Pumps (ASHP): Standalone property-level ASHPs with calorifiers for residential properties.
- Conventional Gas Boilers: Used as a baseline for techno-economic comparison.

## Key Features & Outputs

CATHeaPS 3.0 provides a holistic breakdown of the impacts of residential retrofitting across several metrics:
- Economic Analysis: Detailed breakdown of costs including CAPEX, OPEX, REPEX (Replacement Expenditure), FUELEX (Fuel Expenditure), and specific retrofit costs.
- Environmental Impact: Forecasted CO2 emissions for each thermal system configuration.
- Technical Requirements: Calculation of electrical capacity requirements (in kVA) for the local grid.
- Retrofit Sensitivity: All metrics are presented in "Before" and "After" states based on proposed retrofit measures derived from EPC data.

## Pilot Study: Govanhill, Glasgow

To demonstrate the model’s replicability and accuracy, CATHeaPS has been validated using a pilot project area in Govanhill, Glasgow. This case study serves as a template for other local authorities to assess their bespoke project areas using the same methodology.

## Impact & Just Transition

By lowering the barrier to entry for complex thermal modeling, CATHeaPS supports a just transition by:
- Providing community groups (such as Glasgow Community Energy) with data-driven evidence for heating options.
- Assisting public sector bodies in aligning with the Scottish Government’s Community and Renewable Energy Scheme (CARES).
- Facilitating knowledge exchange through an entirely open-source, interactive framework.

## Requirements
CATHeaPS is a Macro enabled workbook developed on Microsoft Excel.
